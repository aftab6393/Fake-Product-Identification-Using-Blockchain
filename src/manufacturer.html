Quick overview of your flow in the Fake Product Identification system.
  • 1 Use Add Product to create a unique on-chain product entry with a secure ID / QR.
  • 2 Onboard trusted sellers via Add Seller, linking them to your manufacturer identity.
  • 3 Transfer inventory to sellers using Sell Product To Seller to maintain a verifiable supply chain.
  • 4 Use Query Seller to view seller info, product history and ensure authenticity at each hop.
All these actions are recorded via smart contracts, helping downstream consumers detect fake products instantly when they scan the QR.
